# Hypothesis and scope

The project asks whether nonlinear stretching and vorticity in a relativistic
fluid can generate an effective stress-energy contribution capable of producing
macroscopic accelerated expansion. This is a research question, not an
established explanation of cosmology.

The full proposed sequence is in the [research plan](research-plan.md).

## Distinctions to preserve

- Directional stretching is not by itself evidence of increasing volume.
- Increasing volume is not by itself evidence of accelerated expansion.
- A local kinematic diagnostic is not by itself a cosmological prediction.
- A numerical observation needs convergence and physical consistency checks.

## Evidence requirements

Before interpreting any future result, state the model, assumptions, observables,
and criteria that would count against the mechanism. Establish reference cases
and error estimates before exploring unfamiliar regimes. Any transition from
local fluid behavior to a macroscopic model needs an explicit averaging procedure
and a statement of its limits.

A negative result constrains the tested model and parameter range. A surviving
model requires further validation; it does not establish the hypothesis.
